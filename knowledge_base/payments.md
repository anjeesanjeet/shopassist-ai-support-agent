# Payments and pricing

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Loom & Ladle gift cards. We do not accept cash on delivery or cheques.

## Buy now, pay later
Orders between $50 and $1,500 can be split into 4 interest-free payments with Klarna at checkout.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, we refund the difference once per item. Clearance price drops are not eligible.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address.

## Payment security
We never ask for full card numbers, CVV codes or passwords over chat, email or phone.
