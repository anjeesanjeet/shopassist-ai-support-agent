# Shipping policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after your order ships. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders are usually packed and handed to the carrier within 1 business day. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays ship the next business day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $75. Below $75, standard shipping costs $6.95. Express shipping costs $19.95 on any order.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii (add 2 extra business days). We do not ship internationally or to PO boxes for furniture and large decor items.

## Carriers and tracking
We ship with UPS, FedEx and USPS. A tracking number is emailed as soon as your order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delayed or lost packages
If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or a carrier reports a weather or facility delay, contact us and we will open a trace with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost, we send a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
