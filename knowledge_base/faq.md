# Frequently asked questions

## Contact and support hours
Our AI assistant is available 24/7 on web chat and WhatsApp. Human support specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm Eastern Time, and Saturday 9am to 5pm. You can also email support@loomandladle.example.

## Changing an order
Address changes and item changes are possible only while the order is still processing. Once the order ships, changes are no longer possible.

## Order verification
To protect your privacy, we share order details only after you confirm both your order number and the email address used at checkout.

## Gift wrapping
Gift wrapping is available for $5 per item and can be added at checkout. Gift receipts never show prices.

## Product care
Wash cast iron by hand, dry immediately and apply a thin layer of oil. Stoneware mugs and dinner plates are dishwasher and microwave safe. Hand-wash our chef knives and cutting boards.
