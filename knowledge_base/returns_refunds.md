# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, or defective, and include all original parts. Return shipping is free for defective or damaged items; for change-of-mind returns a $7.95 return label fee is deducted from the refund.

## Final sale items
Items marked Clearance or Final Sale cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded, unless they arrive damaged. Damage on final sale items must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos.

## How refunds work
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once a refund is issued, banks usually take 5 to 7 business days to show it. Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit.

## Refund approval limits
Our support assistant can approve refunds of up to $200 instantly for eligible orders. Refunds above $200 are reviewed by a support specialist within 1 business day; the customer does not need to do anything else while the review is in progress.

## Damaged or defective on arrival
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within the 30-day return window, we offer a full refund or a free replacement. Please report it as soon as possible and keep the packaging until the case is closed.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they ship. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the normal return policy.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.
