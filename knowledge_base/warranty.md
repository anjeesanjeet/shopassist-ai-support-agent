# Warranty

## Appliance warranty
All kitchen appliances (blenders, espresso machines, air fryers, kettles and stand mixers) include a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, drops, commercial use, unauthorized repairs, or normal wear such as scratches on non-stick coatings.

## Making a warranty claim
After the 30-day return window, warranty claims are handled as repair or replacement, not refunds. To start a claim, share your order number, a short description of the problem and, if possible, a photo or video. A specialist confirms the claim within 2 business days and emails a prepaid shipping label.

## Cookware and cutlery
Cast iron, enamel cookware and our chef knives carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects. Knife sharpening is not covered.
